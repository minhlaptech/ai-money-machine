1. Automatic Appointment Reminders {#appointment-reminders}

Time saved: 5-10 hours/week

Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

Tools needed: Make.com (affiliate) + Twilio ($0.01/SMS)

The Problem

You or your staff spend hours every day calling or texting customers to remind them about appointments. Some don't show up anyway.

The Solution

Set up an automated system that:

  • Sends a reminder SMS/email 24 hours before each appointment
  • Allows customers to confirm or reschedule with a reply
  • Sends a second reminder 2 hours before
  • Flags no-confirms for manual follow-up

The Impact

Businesses that implement automated reminders see:

  • 30-50% reduction in no-shows
  • 5-10 hours saved per week on manual reminders
  • Higher customer satisfaction (people appreciate reminders)

How to Set It Up

  1. Store appointments in Google Sheets or Google Calendar
  2. Create a Make.com scenario that checks tomorrow's appointments daily
  3. Connect Twilio to send personalized SMS
  4. Add a Google Sheets update step to track confirmations

Cost: ~$12/month for a business with 40 appointments/day.

2. Lead Follow-Up Sequences {#lead-followup}

Time saved: 3-5 hours/week

Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Medium

Tools needed: Make.com (affiliate) + Gmail/Mailchimp

The Problem

A potential customer fills out your contact form at 11 PM. You don't see it until the next morning. By then, they've already called your competitor.

The Solution

Automate your lead follow-up:

  • Instant (within 60 seconds): Send a personalized acknowledgment email
  • 24 hours later: Send helpful resources related to their inquiry
  • 3 days later: Check-in email with a call-to-action
  • 7 days later: Final follow-up with special offer

The Impact

  • 60% faster response time to inquiries
  • 20-30% higher lead-to-customer conversion rate
  • Zero leads fall through the cracks

Pro Tip

Use AI to personalize each email based on what the lead asked about. With Make.com's OpenAI integration, you can analyze the inquiry and customize the response automatically.

3. Customer Support Chatbot {#support-chatbot}

Time saved: 10-20 hours/week

Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Medium

Tools needed: Voiceflow (affiliate) (free tier)

The Problem

You answer the same 20 questions every day. "What are your hours?" "Do you ship to [country]?" "How do I return an item?"

The Solution

Deploy an AI chatbot trained on your FAQs:

  • Answers common questions instantly (24/7)
  • Captures leads when you're offline
  • Hands off complex issues to your team
  • Learns and improves over time

The Impact

  • 40-60% reduction in support tickets reaching your team
  • 24/7 availability (no more lost after-hours inquiries)
  • 3-second response time vs. hours for manual support

4. Social Media Scheduling {#social-media}

Time saved: 4-8 hours/week

Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

Tools needed: Buffer (free) + ChatGPT

The Problem

You know you should post regularly on social media, but creating content is time-consuming and you never stick to a schedule.

The Solution

Batch-create a month of social media content in one sitting:

  1. Use ChatGPT to generate 30 post ideas for your industry
  2. Edit and personalize each post (add your voice)
  3. Schedule everything in Buffer for the month
  4. Automate: best-performing posts get recycled

The Impact

  • Consistent posting without daily effort
  • 4-8 hours saved per week on content creation
  • Better engagement from regular, quality posts

5. Invoice & Payment Reminders {#invoicing}

Time saved: 2-4 hours/week

Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

Tools needed: Zapier (affiliate) + your invoicing tool

The Problem

Chasing payments is awkward and time-consuming. Some clients "forget" to pay for weeks.

The Solution

Automate your payment workflow:

  • Send invoice automatically when project is marked complete
  • Day 3: Friendly reminder if unpaid
  • Day 7: Second reminder with urgency
  • Day 14: Final notice
  • Update your tracking spreadsheet automatically

The Impact

  • 50% faster payment collection
  • Eliminates awkward "just checking in" emails
  • Reduces late payments by 40%

6. Review Collection System {#reviews}

Time saved: 1-2 hours/week

Difficulty: ⭐ Easy

Tools needed: Make.com (affiliate) + SMS/email

The Problem

You know reviews are important, but you never remember to ask for them. And when you do, it feels pushy.

The Solution

Automated review requests:

  • 2 hours after service: "Thank you! How was your experience?"
  • If positive response: Direct link to Google/Yelp review
  • If negative response: Route to private feedback form (damage control)
  • Weekly summary of new reviews sent to you

The Impact

  • 3-5x more reviews per month
  • Higher rating (negative feedback captured privately)
  • Zero effort once set up
