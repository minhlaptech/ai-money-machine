# Chapter 6: AI-Powered Affiliate Marketing
## $300-1000/Month by Recommending Tools You Actually Use

---

Affiliate marketing is the art of earning commissions by recommending products. In 2026, AI makes this incredibly efficient — you can create high-quality content at scale that drives organic traffic and converts readers into buyers.

## How Affiliate Marketing Works

1. You sign up for a company's affiliate program (free)
2. You get a unique tracking link
3. You create content recommending the product
4. Someone clicks your link and buys
5. You earn a commission (typically 20-50% of the sale)

## The AI Advantage

Traditional affiliate marketing required months of content creation before seeing results. With AI:

- **Content creation:** 5x faster with ChatGPT/Claude assistance
- **SEO research:** AI tools identify low-competition keywords
- **Content optimization:** GEO optimization for AI search citations
- **Scale:** Produce 10-20 articles per month instead of 2-3

# Chapter 7: Print-on-Demand with AI Art
## $100-300/Month Semi-Passive Income

---

Print-on-Demand (POD) is the ultimate "set and forget" side income. You create designs, list them on platforms like Etsy or Redbubble, and when someone orders, the platform prints and ships it. You never touch inventory.

With AI art tools, creating professional designs takes minutes instead of hours.

## How POD Works

1. Create a design (AI-assisted)
2. Upload to a POD platform (Printful, Redbubble)
3. List on a marketplace (Etsy, Amazon Merch)
4. Customer orders → Platform prints & ships
5. You earn the profit margin

**Your involvement after listing: zero.**

## The AI Design Workflow

### Tool Options:
- **Midjourney** ($10/month): Best quality AI art
- **DALL-E** (via ChatGPT Plus): Good quality, easy to use
- **Leonardo AI** (free tier): Good for iterating
- **Canva AI** (free): Great for typography + simple designs

### Design Prompt Formula:
```
Create a [style] design for a [product type] about [subject].
Style: [minimalist/vintage/funny/cute/bold]
Colors: [2-3 specific colors]
Background: transparent
Resolution: 4500x5400 pixels
No text in the image.
```

Then add text separately in Canva for full control over typography.

## Growth Strategy

### The Volume Game
POD is a numbers game. More designs = more chances of a sale.

- **Month 1:** Create and list 30-50 designs
- **Month 2:** Add 20-30 more, optimize top performers
- **Month 3:** Hit 100 listings, start running Etsy Ads ($1-3/day)

### The 80/20 Rule
In POD, 20% of your designs will generate 80% of your sales. Once you identify winners:
- Create variations of winning designs
- Expand to more product types
- Target related niches
