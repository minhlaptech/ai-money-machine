## PROMPT 26: Welcome Email Sequence (5 emails)
```
Act as an email marketing expert. Create a 5-email welcome 
sequence for new subscribers to a [BUSINESS TYPE] email list.

Subscriber joined because they downloaded: [LEAD MAGNET]
Goal: Build trust → First sale of [PRODUCT/SERVICE] at $[PRICE]

For each email provide:
- Subject line (+ 2 A/B test alternatives)
- Preview text (max 90 chars)
- Email body (300-500 words)
- CTA button text + URL placeholder
- Send timing (Day 0, Day 1, Day 3, Day 5, Day 7)

Email 1: Welcome + deliver lead magnet
Email 2: Your story + credibility
Email 3: Common mistake in [INDUSTRY]
Email 4: Case study / social proof
Email 5: Soft pitch with special offer

Tone: [Friendly/Professional/Bold]
```

## PROMPT 27: High-Converting Sales Email
```
Write a sales email for [PRODUCT/SERVICE] at $[PRICE].

Reader profile: [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY SIZE]
Pain point: [SPECIFIC PROBLEM]
Main benefit: [#1 OUTCOME]
Urgency: [DEADLINE/SCARCITY]

Use PAS framework (Problem-Agitate-Solution):
- Subject line that achieves >40% open rate (3 alternatives)
- Hook: Relatable problem scenario
- Agitate: Cost of NOT solving (time/money/stress)
- Solution: How [PRODUCT] fixes it
- Proof: One testimonial or stat
- CTA: Single, clear action
- P.S.: Bonus incentive

Max 350 words. Mobile-friendly formatting.
```

## PROMPT 28: Cart Abandonment Recovery Sequence
```
Create a 3-email cart abandonment sequence for a [PRODUCT TYPE] 
e-commerce store. Average cart value: $[X].

Email 1 (1 hour after): Gentle reminder
- Subject: "Did you forget something?"
- Show cart items
- Simple "Complete Purchase" CTA

Email 2 (24 hours after): Address objections
- Subject: "Still thinking about it?"
- Address top 3 objections for [PRODUCT TYPE]
- Add social proof (reviews/ratings)
- Free shipping or small discount hint

Email 3 (72 hours after): Last chance
- Subject: "Your cart is expiring..."
- Create urgency (items selling fast)
- Offer 10% discount code
- "Last chance" CTA

Include subject line alternatives for each.
```

## PROMPT 35: B2B Cold Outreach Email
```
Write a cold outreach email to [JOB TITLE] at [COMPANY SIZE] 
companies in the [INDUSTRY] space.

My service: [WHAT YOU OFFER]
Key benefit: [PRIMARY VALUE PROPOSITION]
Social proof: [NOTABLE CLIENTS/RESULTS]

Requirements:
- Subject line under 8 words (+ 3 alternatives)
- Under 150 words total
- NO "I hope this finds you well"
- Start with THEIR problem, not your pitch
- One specific, relevant insight about their business
- Soft CTA (not "buy now" — "would it make sense to chat?")
- Include a P.S. with one more proof point

Write 3 variations:
1. Pain-point focused
2. Curiosity-driven
3. Social-proof led
```
